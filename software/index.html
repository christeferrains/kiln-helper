    Witness cones

    Put a cone where you can see it through the peephole. Real cones tell you what the kiln really did.

    • Just right: the tip bent over until it's even with the bottom.
    • Not hot enough: it's still standing up or only leaning.
    • Too hot: it's flat or melted onto the shelf.

    Something wrong?

    The temperature isn't going up
    • The kiln's dial switches are turned all the way up to High.
    • The kiln sitter has a cone in it, its button is pushed in, and its timer is set.
    • The kiln is plugged into the controller box.
    • The red E-stop isn't pressed. Twist it to let it pop back out.
    • Run the Heat test above.
    The kiln turned off partway through
    • The kiln sitter tripped: the cone in it was too cool. Use the hotter cone the start checklist shows.
    • The kiln sitter's timer ran out. Set it longer than the firing (the start checklist shows how long). It only goes to 20 hours.
    • The E-stop was pressed, or the high-limit cut the power.
    It heats too slowly
    • Elements wear out after many firings and heat slower. A kiln tech can test them.
    • Make sure the lid and peephole plugs are closed (after the preheat).
    • The controller waits for a slow kiln, so the firing may take longer than planned.
    It goes hotter than the goal
    • The controller needs tuning. A grown-up can run kiln-controller's autotune with the kiln empty.
    The temperature shows -- or a crazy number
    • The thermocouple plug came loose. Check the yellow plug on the bottom of the box.
    • The thermocouple tip may be broken or touching an element. Turn everything off and have a grown-up look.
    Pots cracked or blew up
    • They were still damp. Water turns to steam and pushes the clay apart.
    • Next time pick Raw greenware in "Fire by cone" and a longer preheat.
    • During the preheat, hold a mirror at the peephole. If it fogs, tap Add 30 minutes.
    The cones don't match the screen
    • Thermocouples drift with age. Fire a witness cone and compare.
    • A grown-up can set a correction (offset) in kiln-controller's settings file.
    "Lost connection"
    • The firing keeps going on the controller. Only this screen lost touch.
    • Check the Wi-Fi, then refresh the page.
